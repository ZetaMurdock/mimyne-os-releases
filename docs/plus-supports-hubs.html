Plus supports Hubs: program terms

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Part of every Mimyne Plus subscription goes to the Hubs its member actually uses. Owners sell nothing, and members pay nothing extra. These terms say how that works and what a Hub owner agrees to when their Hub earns. They are part of the Terms of Service, and Wonderma Corporation ("Mimyne", "we") runs the program.

1. What the program is, and is not

  • The program is something Mimyne runs and can change. It is not a promise of revenue, a partnership, an employment relationship or a joint venture. A Hub owner who receives payments is an independent contractor.
  • Only money a member paid for their own Mimyne Plus subscription funds the program. Hub Pro and gifted Plus never do.
  • Nothing in the program changes who owns a Hub, its content or its members' content.

2. How a member's support is divided

  • Each month, up to 30% of the net revenue from a member's Plus subscription (what Mimyne receives after the seller's fees, taxes, refunds and chargebacks) becomes that member's support budget. We may raise this share; we will not lower it without telling members and owners at least 30 days ahead.
  • The budget is divided only among the eligible Hubs the member spent qualified time in that month, in proportion to that time. It never goes into a shared pot.
  • Time in a Hub that is not eligible, or outside Hubs, is not assigned to anyone and stays with Mimyne. That is why the share is "up to" 30%.
  • A yearly subscription counts as one twelfth of its net revenue in each of its twelve months.
  • A member may instead choose to have their support split evenly between the Hubs they pledge to (Settings > Your support). Their minutes are then not recorded at all.

3. Qualified time

  • A minute counts when the Hub's tab is on screen and the person did something in the last two minutes (scrolled, typed, clicked, reacted, moved something on a Canvas), or a video or Clip is playing in the foreground.
  • A minute counts at most once per person, across all Hubs, whatever tab or device sent it.
  • For each person, in each Hub, each day: the first 60 minutes count in full, the next 120 count half, and anything past three hours counts nothing.
  • The Hub owner's own minutes do not count. Nor do minutes from accounts less than seven days old, accounts without a verified email address, or accounts we have flagged for abuse.
  • We may change how minutes are counted to keep the split fair and to stop abuse.

4. Which Hubs can earn

A Hub can earn in a month when, as the month ends, all of these hold. We may adjust the numbers; we will publish any change here.

  • The Hub is at least 30 days old.
  • Its owner is 18 or older and has been verified through our payout provider.
  • At least 5 distinct Plus members contributed qualified time to it that month.
  • At least 15 distinct people were active in it in the last 28 days.
  • The Hub has no open strikes and no unresolved reports against it under the Community Guidelines.

Amounts a Hub would have received while it was not eligible are not held for it; they stay with Mimyne.

5. The monthly close, holds and payouts

  • A month's earnings are worked out on the 5th of the following month and added to the Hub's balance. Until then, what an owner sees is an estimate.
  • A new owner's first earnings are held for 90 days. After six months with no reversals or disputes, the hold drops to 30 days. The hold covers the window in which a card payment can be disputed.
  • Payouts are made monthly through our payout provider (Stripe Connect) once the released balance reaches US $25. Smaller balances carry over; nothing is lost. Mimyne pays the provider's fees, so owners receive the full amount.
  • Payouts are available in the countries our payout provider supports for this kind of account. We list them when setting up a payout account.
  • If an owner never finishes setting up a payout account, we keep the balance for 12 months. After that it is forfeited to Mimyne.

6. Refunds, disputes and reversals

  • Money from a Plus payment that is refunded or disputed is taken back out of the balance of every Hub it funded, even if that leaves a balance negative for a while. A negative balance is recovered from later earnings.
  • We may hold, reduce or cancel earnings that come from abuse of the program, from accounts that break the Terms of Service or the Community Guidelines, or from a mistake in counting or payment.

7. What owners and members must not do

  • Do not ask, pressure or pay members to leave a Hub open, idle in it or otherwise inflate time. "Keep this tab open to support us" is against the Community Guidelines and earns a strike.
  • Do not buy Plus with stolen payment details, use extra accounts, or arrange with others to move support around. Accounts tied to the same payout identity, device or payment method may be linked and dropped from the count.
  • Do not try to work out which members contributed or how much any one person gave.

8. Taxes and records

  • Owners are responsible for any tax on what they receive. Our payout provider collects the tax details it needs (such as a W-9 or W-8BEN) when a payout account is set up, and we file the forms the law requires.
  • We keep records of earnings, holds, reversals and payouts for as long as tax and accounting rules require.

9. Privacy

What is counted, what is kept and for how long, and who can see what, are in the Privacy Policy under "Plus supports Hubs". In short: owners see counts and amounts, never who; members see only their own split; nothing is sold or used for ads.

10. Changes and ending the program

  • We may change these terms, the share, the counting rules and the eligibility rules. Changes that reduce what Hubs receive take effect no sooner than 30 days after we post them here, except where needed to stop abuse or to comply with the law.
  • We may pause or end the program. Earnings already added to a balance are still paid out under these terms.
