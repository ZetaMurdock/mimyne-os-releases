Community Guidelines

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Mimyne is a place people build in and show to each other. These guidelines say what is and isn't allowed anywhere other people can see you, what happens when someone breaks the rules, and how to report or appeal. They sit alongside the Terms of Service — the Terms are the agreement, this is how it is enforced.

The short version
  • Don't harass, threaten, hate on, or impersonate people.
  • Nothing sexual involving minors, ever. That one is permanent and reported.
  • No porn, no gore, no malware, no scams.
  • Report from the profile, comment or room where it happened. Block anyone, any time, for any reason.
  • Most first breaks get a warning. Repeats climb: strike, limits, suspension, ban.

1. Where these rules apply

Anywhere in Mimyne that another person can see: your username and display name, your profile page and its banner, bio, pinned note, songs and clips, comments you leave, workspaces you share and anything you put in them, and — as they arrive — messages you send and anything you post in a Room or Hub.

What stays on your own computer and is shared with nobody is yours. We don't look at it, and these rules don't reach it.

2. What isn't allowed

  • Sexual content involving minors. Any of it, drawn or real, in any form. This is a permanent ban on the first offence and is reported to the authorities.
  • Harassment. Targeting someone, piling on, following them around Mimyne, sexual harassment, or encouraging others to do it.
  • Threats and violence. Threatening harm to anyone, celebrating violence, or promoting a violent group.
  • Hate. Attacking people over race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, disease, age, sex, gender identity or sexual orientation.
  • Doxxing. Posting someone's private information — address, phone number, workplace, accounts, documents — or threatening to.
  • Impersonation. Pretending to be another person, a brand, or Mimyne staff.
  • Explicit content. Mimyne is open to people from 13 up, so pornography and real gore aren't allowed anywhere others can see them. Suggestive art, swearing, dark humour, mature themes and video-game violence are all fine — the line is explicit sexual content and real-world gore, not edge.
  • Self-harm. Encouraging or instructing anyone to hurt themselves. If you are struggling, talk to someone — Mimyne is not a crisis service, and the people here are not obliged to carry it for you.
  • Scams, spam and malware. Fake giveaways, phishing, mass unsolicited messages, harmful automations or files, or anything meant to take an account or a machine.
  • Illegal goods and services. Drugs, weapons, stolen accounts or data, forged documents.
  • Other people's work. Don't post what you don't have the right to post.
  • Breaking Mimyne itself. Getting into accounts, data or workspaces that aren't yours, getting around access rules or bans, scraping, or overloading the service.

Rules don't cover everything. Something can be wrong here without appearing on this list, and we'll say so plainly when we act on it.

3. What happens when someone breaks them

Most things are a mistake or a bad day, and are dealt with quietly. Repeats and cruelty are not.

  • A warning. The content comes down and you're told which rule it broke. Nothing else happens.
  • A strike. On your account, with the reason and a date it expires. Strikes fall off on their own if nothing else happens.
  • Limits. With strikes standing, you keep your account and your work but can't comment, post or message for a set time.
  • Suspension. You can't sign in for a set time.
  • A ban. Permanent, and for the person, not the account — a new account to get around a ban is banned too.

Serious things skip the ladder. Sexual content involving minors, credible threats of violence, doxxing campaigns, and attacks on other people's accounts are permanent on the first offence.

You are always told what happened and why. Losing access to your workspaces isn't part of the punishment: what's on your own computer stays yours, and a suspension doesn't delete it.

4. Reporting

Report from the place it happened — a profile, a comment, a clip, a shared workspace, or a message. Say what rule it broke and, if it isn't obvious, what we're looking at. Reports are read by hand.

Today that hand is the developer's. As Mimyne grows, reports go to a small group of auditors who see what they need for the level they work at, and every action any of them takes is written down and kept.

  • We never tell the person you reported that it was you.
  • Reporting somebody out of spite, or mass-reporting to silence someone, is itself a breach.
  • An emergency is not a report. If someone is in immediate danger, call your local emergency services first.

Blocking is yours to use whenever you like and needs no reason: a blocked person can't reach you, and you don't have to justify it to anybody.

5. Appeals

If we got it wrong, say so: email mimyne.support@gmail.com from the address on your account with your username, what happened, and why you think it was a mistake. One appeal per action, answered by someone other than whoever made the original call where that's possible. We aim to answer within a few days; we are a small operation and sometimes it takes longer.

Appeals of a permanent ban for sexual content involving minors are not entertained.

6. Rooms, Hubs and your own space

When Hubs and Rooms arrive, whoever runs one may set stricter rules than these for their own space, and enforce them there. Nobody can set looser ones: these guidelines are the floor everywhere in Mimyne.

A workspace you share is yours to run. You choose who gets in and what they can do, and you can remove anyone's notes or comments from it. That doesn't override these rules — what's not allowed in Mimyne isn't allowed in your room either.

7. Copyright

If something in Mimyne infringes your copyright, email mimyne.support@gmail.com with a link to it, what it copies, proof you hold the rights, and a statement that you believe in good faith the use isn't authorised. We take down what infringes. If yours came down and shouldn't have, tell us the same way.

8. Changes

These guidelines will change as Mimyne grows — messages, Rooms and Hubs each bring their own problems. We'll change the date above, and say so in the app when a change is significant.
